# TestFlight: "What to Test"

Paste the text below into the build's **What to Test** box (App Store Connect → YoungVista →
**TestFlight** → the build). Testers see it in the TestFlight app. This one is for Build 1 of
the plan (scan a room and save it); change it when a build adds something new.

```
Thanks for testing YoungVista: scan a room with your iPhone and get its floor plan.

PLEASE TRY
1. Sign in: Create an account with your email and a password, or tap Continue with Google.
2. Tap Scan a room and allow the camera. Point the phone at the floor until it is found, then turn slowly around the room, aiming at the bottom of each wall. Walls appear on the little map as you turn.
3. A wall it misses: aim the dot at the bottom edge of that wall, tap Mark wall, then aim at the other end of the wall and tap again.
4. The bug button (top right) shows the debug overlay: the planes the phone found, and a red, a green and a blue line for each wall. Which line runs along the bottom of the real wall? Tap Share the raw data once and send it to the team.
5. When the walls go all the way round, the room closes by itself (or tap Done). Check the ceiling height, tap Next, name the room and tap Save room.
6. On a computer, open https://young-vista.web.app and sign in with the same account: is the room there, with the same plan and wall lengths?
7. Anything that looked wrong, was slow or confused you. With a tape measure, compare one wall with the app's length.

HOW TO REPORT
In the TestFlight app: YoungVista → Send Beta Feedback (you can add a screenshot), or tell the team. Say which room and what happened.
```
