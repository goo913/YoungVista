# TestFlight: "What to Test"

Paste the text below into the build's **What to Test** box (App Store Connect → YoungVista →
**TestFlight** → the build). Testers see it in the TestFlight app. This one is for Build 1 of
the plan (scan a room and save it); change it when a build adds something new.

```
Thanks for testing YoungVista: scan a room with your iPhone and get its floor plan. Each scan sends the team a small technical log (no photos).

PLEASE TRY
1. Sign in: Create an account with your email and a password, or tap Continue with Google.
2. Tap Scan a room and allow the camera. Stand near the middle of the room and point the phone at the floor until it is found.
3. First wall: aim the dot where a wall meets the floor and tap Mark. Then aim further along the same wall (further is better) and tap Mark again.
4. Ceiling: aim where that wall meets the ceiling and tap Mark the top, or tap Skip, use 8 ft.
5. Turn to each other wall, face it, aim at its bottom edge (or its top edge if furniture is in the way) and tap Mark. Each wall shows in the room as lines on the floor and ceiling. A wrong mark turns walls red: tap Undo.
6. When every wall is marked the room closes by itself and shows its size. Tap Next. If you measured a wall with a tape, type it under "Measured a wall with a tape?". Name the room and tap Save room.
7. On a computer, open https://young-vista.web.app and sign in with the same account: is the room there, with the same plan and wall lengths?
8. Anything that looked wrong, was slow or confused you. With a tape measure, compare one wall with the app's length.

HOW TO REPORT
In the TestFlight app: YoungVista → Send Beta Feedback (you can add a screenshot), or tell the team. Say which room and what happened.
```
